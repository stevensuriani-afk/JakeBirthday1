Enter your first name and password to play for birthday money.

HOW TO PLAY

Each colored box represents a word or a few words.

When you press SUBMIT, you will hear the words in the order the boxes are currently arranged.

The words make a familiar phrase — but they start out jumbled!

Drag the colored boxes left or right until you think the words are in the correct order. Press SUBMIT again to hear your new arrangement. You can keep rearranging the boxes and trying again.

There is no time limit for an individual phrase. Use QUIT THIS PUZZLE to reveal and hear the correct order. The answer stays visible until you click NEXT PUZZLE.

IMPORTANT: You can enter Real Money Mode only once. Starting it uses your one opportunity, even if you leave the game early. Make sure you have 30 uninterrupted minutes available before you begin.

Practice Mode contains 12 puzzles that you can replay as often as you like.

In Real Money Mode, you have 30 minutes total to solve as many phrases as possible. Each correct phrase earns $2.50. Real Money Mode ends at $50, 20 puzzles, or 30 minutes, whichever comes first.
